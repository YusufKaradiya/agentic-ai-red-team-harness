# Research Questions

## Primary Research Question

How effectively can a lightweight layered defense approach reduce prompt injection, tool abuse and synthetic sensitive-data leakage in a simulated tool-enabled AI agent while maintaining normal task utility?

## Secondary Research Questions

### RQ1

How does the proposed defensive workflow affect attack success rate compared with an unprotected baseline?

### RQ2

Can tool permission controls reduce unauthorized tool usage in simulated AI-agent workflows?

### RQ3

How effectively can input and output checks reduce synthetic sensitive-data leakage?

### RQ4

What is the impact of security controls on false-block rate and normal task utility?

### RQ5

Which attack categories remain most difficult to detect after applying the proposed controls?

## Hypothesis

A lightweight layered defense consisting of input inspection, threat-informed attack testing, tool permission checks and output leakage detection will reduce successful attacks and sensitive-data leakage compared with an unprotected baseline, while maintaining acceptable normal-task utility.
